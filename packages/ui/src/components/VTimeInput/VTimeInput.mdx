# TimeInput

A time picker: a [VInput](?path=/docs/components-input--docs) field and, optionally, a
floating panel. Three ways of filling the field, chosen through the `mode` prop — a
**masked entry**, a **picker** or a **list of times**.

**By default the time is typed on the keyboard and there is no panel**: `mode` is `input`
and `showPicker` is `false`.

The shell is the [VDateInput](?path=/docs/components-dateinput--docs) one: a
`popover="manual"` panel anchored in **pure CSS** (`anchor-scope` on the root +
`anchor-name` on the field's own box → `position-anchor`), a programmatic opening that
moves focus into the panel, and closing through the root's `focusout` + `Escape`. The
anchor is the field and not the component's root, which also holds the label and the hint:
the panel opens against the field, and covers the hint while it is open.

Whatever the mode, the **v-model stays `'HH:mm'` in canonical 24 h** (`null` when empty):
picking 7 PM produces `'19:00'`.

## Three field modes

- **`input`** (the default): the field is typed on the keyboard, behind an "HH:MM" mask.
  The picker is optional (`showPicker`) and absent by default.
- **`picker`**: the field is filled from the clock alone, which becomes the only route —
  `showPicker` is therefore forced to `true` there.
- **`list`**: a `VCombobox` offers the times in steps of `minuteStep`, searchable by
  typing; `showPicker` is beside the point there.

## What may be chosen

**`min`** and **`max`** (inclusive bounds, canonical `'HH:mm'`) and **`allowedHours`** and
**`allowedMinutes`** (a list of the values allowed, or a rule answering for one) restrict the value.
They are handed to VTimePicker as they stand, and the answer follows from what the mode ASKS the
reader for — a value to pick, or a value to write:

- **`list`** and **`picker`**, the two that OFFER the times: what cannot be chosen is left out. A
  list and a face are both read before they are chosen from, and neither has anything to say with a
  row or a numeral that exists only to be refused. The list keeps the row of the value in force even
  when the restrictions have moved past it, since the field names a value through the row that
  carries it; see the picker's own page for how a bound cuts an hour in half rather than closing it.
- **`input`**, the one that asks the reader to WRITE: what is typed is committed, and the field
  turns invalid. It is carried by the control's own validity, so the field goes red through
  `:user-invalid` once the reader has interacted with it, and a form refuses to leave with it.
  Swallowing the entry instead would give the reader nothing to correct.

## Masked entry (the default)

The user types **digits only**: the colon is placed as soon as the hour is complete.
Unlike VDateInput's mask, whose field order and separator derive from the locale, this
one is **universal** — `Intl` only varies the hour cycle, never the colon.

Entry rules:

- typing a separator is tolerated and completes the hour with a leading zero ("9:30" gives
  the same result as "0930"),
- `Backspace` on the colon erases the digit preceding it,
- an incomplete or impossible entry ("25:00") **silently reverts** to the current value on
  leaving the field; emptying the field sets the value to `null`,
- `Enter` confirms and closes, so it does not submit the surrounding form.

`showPicker` adds the end-of-field icon and the picker: the panel then opens on focus
**without taking focus**, typing carries on in the field, and the `down arrow` stays the
explicit route to the picker.

`pickerIcon` replaces that icon and only that one: `mode="list"` is a VCombobox and takes
its chevron, its cross and their wording from there. `clearable`'s cross sits **to the left**
of the picker icon without replacing it.

## The picker

Two buttons display the draft time (hour / minutes) and choose the active step: the one
for the step under way switches to the accent tone. On the picker, a click or a drag
positions the hand; releasing after the hour has been chosen moves on to the minutes
automatically. `minuteStep` imposes a granularity, and the face prints only the
minutes it reaches: at the default of one minute it marks every five, which is what a
clock is read on, where a quarter of an hour marks four.

The panel works on a **draft**: only **OK** writes the v-model; **Cancel**, `Escape` and
focus leaving abandon the selection under way — you can feel your way around the picker
before confirming.

The `#footer` slot **replaces** those two buttons rather than joining them, and it receives both
actions — `{ confirm, cancel }`. That is what makes it usable: the clock writes a draft, so a
footer of your own without `confirm` would leave the value unchangeable through the panel. It is
not rendered in the list form, which has no panel of its own.

In 24 h, the picker carries a **double ring**: 12 and 1–11 on the outside, 00 and 13–23 on
the inside.

The numerals and the hand are positioned in **pure CSS** — `sin()`/`cos()` trigonometry
(CSS Values 4, well below the design system's browser floor) on a turn fraction set as an
inline variable. The JS is limited to the pointer → value conversion and the keyboard.

## The list of times

The `list` mode enumerates the day in steps of `minuteStep` (15 or 30 minutes are the
expected values; a finer step is flagged in development — at a 1-minute step the list
would be 1440 rows). On opening, the panel is scrolled onto the current value.

It **is a `VCombobox`**, so a time is found by typing as well as by scrolling, and the
whole listbox pattern — the highlight, the keyboard, the chevron, the clear cross — is
that component's. The search takes the words as they are written, "PM" and ":30" included,
and it also takes the **bare digit run**: "930" finds half past nine without the colon
being typed, which is how a time is entered in the typed form too.

Choosing a time **commits immediately and closes**: picking from a list is a single
gesture, so there is neither a draft nor an OK button here. A search matching no row
leaves the value alone — the list is what may be chosen, and a time outside it belongs to
the `input` mode. A value the consumer sets off the step is the one exception: it joins
the rows in its place, so the field can write it out and the panel can open on it.

## The 12 h / 24 h format and AM/PM

The hour cycle **derives from the locale** (`Intl`'s `hourCycle`) and is overridable
through the `format` prop. The picker itself goes back to a single ring.

In 12 h, the **typed form** shows an AM/PM button **inside the field**, beside the value
it qualifies and before the controls that clear the field and open the panel. It writes
the v-model directly, with nothing to open or confirm: typing "9:30" says nothing about
the morning or the afternoon, so the answer has to be readable and changeable with the
panel shut. It is the only place a 12 h field needs one. The `picker` form reaches the
clock, which carries its own AM/PM; and every row of the `list` form already spells its
half of the day out.

As long as no time is set, the chosen meridiem is simply **remembered** and will apply to
the first time entered.

The field displays the value formatted by `Intl` according to the locale.

## States

The field follows the input-component scale — `sm` (32px), `md` (40px, the default) and
`lg` (48px), combinable with `compact` (-4px); the panel keeps its dimensions — takes
`clearable` (the cross then appears to the left of the end icon as soon as a value is
set, including during an entry under way) and supports `disabled`. Cancel always
restores the last committed value.

`readonly` freezes the time whichever way it could have been changed: nothing can be typed, no
clock is rendered, the AM/PM button goes since it writes the value, and the clear cross goes with
them, along with the attributes that announced a panel. It reaches the list form too, that one
being a VCombobox. Unlike `disabled`, the field keeps its contrast, takes the focus and can be
copied from. It answers a different question from `mode`, which says how a field that **can** be
changed is filled in.

`loading` shows a spinner in place of the clock icon while something is being fetched, and changes
nothing else. `iconStart` puts an icon at the start of the field, decorative until a
`@click:icon-start` listener turns it into a button — which then needs `iconStartLabel`.
`pickerIconLabel`, `clearLabel` and `loadingLabel` rename the clock button, the clear cross and the
spinner when the dictionary's wording is not the right one.

## Accessibility

- With a picker, the field becomes a `role="combobox"` carrying `aria-haspopup="dialog"`,
  `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` — a plain text field does not support
  `aria-expanded`. An input field with no `showPicker` keeps its native `textbox` role and
  announces no popup. The `list` mode is a VCombobox and carries that component's contract
  whole.
- **Opening** on click, on `Enter` or on the `down arrow` from the field. In `picker`
  focus moves into the panel at once; when typing with `showPicker`, the panel opens
  **without taking focus**.
- The picker is a **single `role="slider"`** (one tab stop): arrows for ±1 hour or
  ±`minuteStep` minutes, `PageUp`/`PageDown` for ±5 minutes, `Home`/`End` for the bounds,
  `Enter` to settle the step. The picker's numerals are purely decorative (`aria-hidden`);
  the value is announced through `aria-valuetext` and the step change through a live
  region.
- The list is a VCombobox, so **focus never leaves the field**: the arrows and `Home`/`End`
  move `aria-activedescendant` through the rows and `Enter` takes the highlighted one. That
  is what lets a reader keep typing to narrow the list while a row is highlighted.
- The AM/PM button is named for its role **and** the half of the day it currently shows,
  "AM or PM: PM". The name therefore repeats the visible word rather than replacing it, so
  a reader arriving on the button is told which half is chosen and hears the new one as
  soon as it changes. It sits before the clear cross and the end icon in the DOM, which is
  what keeps the reading order and the tab order in agreement.
- **Closing** through `Escape` or Cancel (focus returns to the field, with no commit),
  through OK, or — in `list` mode — by choosing a time.
- Since a placeholder is not an accessible description, describe the expected format
  through `hint` (linked to the field by `aria-describedby`).
- The picker is **not mirrored in RTL**: a clock is a universal object and clockwise is
  invariant; the HH:MM group likewise forces the hour–minutes visual order. Transitions
  respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

This component's accessible labels come from the design system dictionary: they are
translated globally (see **Foundations › Internationalization**). The `locale` prop stays
authoritative; without it, the component follows the global locale set by `setLocale`.
