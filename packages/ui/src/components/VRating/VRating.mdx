# Rating

A rating out of a few icons. VRating is a group of native radios, one per icon, so the arrow keys,
form submission and `required` are the browser's own.

<Canvas of={VRatingStories.Default} />

<Controls of={VRatingStories.Default} />

## Value

`v-model` holds a whole number from 1 to `max` (5 by default), or `null` when there is no rating.
Hovering previews the rating the click would give.

## Clearing

By default, a rating can change but not go back to none, as in any group of radios. `clearable`
lets it: clicking the current value again clears it, and a "No rating" choice comes first, so the
keyboard can reach it with the arrows. While it has the focus, the ring surrounds the whole row.

<Canvas of={VRatingStories.Clearable} />

## Read-only

`readonly` shows the value without letting it change. The icons become a single image named
after the value, such as "3.7 out of 5", and a fractional value fills part of an icon, from the
start of the line. With a `name`, the value is still sent in a form.

## Tones and icons

`tone` colours the filled icons, `accent` by default. `color` replaces the tone with a colour of
your own (hex, CSS name or `oklch()`), whose contrast with the background is yours to check.

`icon` replaces the star; the empty icons are drawn in outline and the filled ones with the icon's
filled form. The built-in `star` goes through the icon resolver, so `setIconResolver` can map it
to your own set everywhere.

<Canvas of={VRatingStories.TonesAndIcons} />

## Field

VRating follows the field model: `label` becomes the legend of its `<fieldset>`, `hint` describes
the group, and `error` replaces the hint, is announced, marks every radio `aria-invalid` and draws
the empty icons in red. `invalid` does the same without a message, for a rule of your own.
`required` adds an asterisk and makes the browser require a choice.

## Accessibility

- The group is a `<fieldset>` named by its legend, or "Rating" from the dictionary without a
  `label`. Each radio is named "3 out of 5", whatever the icon.
- The radios are native and hidden, never with `display: none`: they keep the focus, the arrow
  keys and form submission. The focus ring is drawn on the icon.
- Clearing by clicking again is limited to the pointer; the keyboard clears through "No rating",
  so Space never undoes a choice.
- Read-only, the icons are one `role="img"` whose name gives the value, formatted for the locale.
- In forced colours, the filled icons take the system highlight colour.
