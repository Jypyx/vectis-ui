# Calendar

An inline calendar (a grid), Material-inspired. It carries **all the logic** of selection, of
navigation by views (days / months / years) and of the keyboard; the
[VDatePicker](?path=/docs/components-datepicker--docs) merely dresses it in a field and a floating
panel.

The platform offers no accessible, composable date grid (`<input type="date">` is neither stylable
nor decomposable): the JS therefore implements the ARIA **grid** pattern — roving tabindex, arrows,
`PageUp`/`PageDown`, single / range / multiple selection. Everything is computed in **local time**
through ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` strings (no timezone drift, SSR-safe).

## Selection modes

`selection` accepts `single` (the default), `range` and `multiple`. The v-model's type follows the
selection: `string | null`, `{ start, end }` (`DateRange`), or `string[]`.

## min / max bounds

`min` and `max` (ISO) lock the navigation **and** the selection: the months outside the range are
no longer reachable and the days outside it are disabled.

<Canvas of={VCalendarStories.MinMax} />

## Disabled dates

`disabledDates` accepts an array of ISO strings **or** a predicate `(iso) => boolean`. The dates
concerned are struck through and non-selectable (but stay keyboard-focusable, as the ARIA pattern
requires).

<Canvas of={VCalendarStories.DisabledDates} />

## Adjacent months

`showAdjacentDays` (off by default) displays the days of the previous/next months in grey — enable
it by mere presence: `<VCalendar show-adjacent-days />`. `selectAdjacentDays` makes them clickable
(and implies displaying them) — a click then navigates to their month and selects them.

<Canvas of={VCalendarStories.AdjacentMonths} />

## Events

`events` (`{ date, color?, label? }[]`) displays up to three coloured dots under a date.

<Canvas of={VCalendarStories.Events} />

## Custom content (the `#day` slot)

The `#day` slot replaces a cell's content — a ticket price under the day number, for instance. It
receives `{ iso, day, inMonth, disabled, selected, today, inRange, events }`.

For content on several lines, enlarge the dot through the public CSS variable
**`--vectis-calendar-day-size`** (default `2rem`) — the example below uses 40 px dots and always
renders the price line (empty when absent) to keep the numbers aligned from one cell to the next.

The slot also applies to the days of the adjacent months (`showAdjacentDays`), so their number stays
aligned with the days of the month: use `inMonth` to display the data only on the days of the
displayed month, while keeping the same content height.

## Localization

`locale` drives the month/day names and the first day of the week (derived from the locale,
overridable through `firstDayOfWeek`). The labels go through `Intl`, which is available on the

## Accessibility

- The days grid is a `role="grid"` named by the month and the year; the column headers carry the
  full day name (`aria-label`).
- **Roving tabindex**: a single cell is tabbable; the selection and "today" are signalled by
  `aria-selected` and `aria-current="date"`.
- **Keyboard**: arrows (± 1 day / ± 1 week), `Home`/`End` (the week's bounds), `PageUp`/`PageDown`
  (± 1 month, `Shift` → ± 1 year), `Enter`/`Space` (selection). The month and year views are
  navigable with the arrows too.
- The visible focus uses the design system's standard focus ring; every transition respects
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.

This component's accessible labels come from the design system dictionary: they translate globally
(see **Foundations › Internationalization**). The `locale` prop stays authoritative; without it, the
component follows the global locale set by `setLocale`.
