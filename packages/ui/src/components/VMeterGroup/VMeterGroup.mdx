# MeterGroup

Several measurements sharing one range, drawn as the parts of a single bar with a legend: the
storage taken by each kind of file, a budget split into expenses. Where VMeter shows one reading,
VMeterGroup shows how a total breaks down.

## Items and range

`items` lists the parts in the order they fill the bar, each with a `label` and a `value` on the
scale of `max`. What the parts leave of the range stays empty. When they add up to more than
`max`, they share the whole bar in proportion and the legend keeps their real amounts. A negative
amount, or one that is not a number, counts as zero and draws no part.

<Canvas of={VMeterGroupStories.Overflow} />

## Colours

Each item takes a `tone` or a `color` of your own. Without either, its hue is the accent's turned
by the golden angle for its position, which keeps neighbouring parts apart for any number of
items; the first part is the accent itself. Lightness and chroma stay the accent's, so the derived
colours follow the theme. The legend swatch, or the item's `icon`, takes the same colour.

<Canvas of={VMeterGroupStories.Colours} />

## Values

`showValue` writes the sum of the amounts after the label. The sum and every amount are
percentages of `max` by default; `formatOptions` formats them for the current locale with
`Intl.NumberFormat`, and `valueText` replaces the sum with words of your own.

<Canvas of={VMeterGroupStories.Formatted} />

The `value` slot replaces the sum and receives `{ value, max, percent }`. The `item-leading`,
`item-label` and `item-value` slots replace a legend row's swatch, label and amount, and receive
`{ item, index, percent }`. What `item-leading` renders is decorative and hidden from screen
readers.

<Canvas of={VMeterGroupStories.Slots} />

## Sizes

`size` sets the thickness of the bar on the scale of VMeter and VProgressLinear: `xs`, `sm`, `md`
(the default), `lg` and `xl`. The bar takes the whole width available.

<Canvas of={VMeterGroupStories.Sizes} />

## Vertical

`orientation="vertical"` fills the bar from the bottom up and puts the legend beside it. A height
on the component sets the bar's length.

<Canvas of={VMeterGroupStories.Vertical} />

## Right to left

The bar fills from the reading start.

## Accessibility

- ARIA has no meter holding several values. The bar is therefore hidden from assistive
  technologies, and the legend, a list whose rows give each label and amount in text, carries the
  reading. `hideLegend` hides it visually only.
- The root is a `group` named by `label`, or by the dictionary's generic name (`meterGroup.label`)
  without one; an `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` of your own wins over both. The sum written by
  `showValue` is read as ordinary text.
- Colour tells the parts apart on screen but never alone: every part has its row in the legend.
- In forced colours, the parts are painted in the system highlight colour and kept apart by their
  gaps; the legend still names them.
- The parts' movement is removed when reduced motion is requested.
