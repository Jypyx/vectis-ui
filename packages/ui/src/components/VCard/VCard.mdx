# Card

A surface that groups the content about one subject: a media, a title and subtitle, a body and a
footer of actions. Each part is optional, and a part with no content leaves no empty space. With
`href`, the whole card becomes a link.

## Variants

`outline`, the default, draws a border on the page surface. `elevated` lifts a raised surface with
a shadow. `filled` uses the muted surface with no border, for cards inside a page that already
has a lot of lines. `flat` draws nothing and lets the surrounding surface show through; a linked
flat card still takes the muted surface on hover.

<Canvas of={VCardStories.Variants} />

## Sizes

`size` sets the padding and the gaps between the parts, from `sm` for dense lists to `lg` for a
featured card. The typography does not change.

<Canvas of={VCardStories.Sizes} />

## Horizontal

`orientation="horizontal"` places the media at the start of the content. When the card is
narrower than the media (`--vectis-control-size-card-media`) and the content
(`--vectis-control-size-card-body-min`) together, the media moves back above the content. This
uses wrapping, not a container query, so the card keeps working inside a parent that sizes it
from its content.

<Canvas of={VCardStories.Horizontal} />

## Linked cards

With `href`, the title becomes a real `<a>` whose clickable area covers the card. A screen reader
hears one link, named by the title, instead of all the card's text. Buttons and links placed in
the card are raised above that area and keep their own behaviour.

The attributes set on a linked card, except `class` and `style`, go to the link: this is where
`target`, `rel` or a router's click handler belong. Without `href`, they all stay on the root.

`headingLevel` turns the title into a heading, so that a grid of cards can be browsed by heading.
The cards in a grid stretch to the height of their row, and their footers line up at the bottom.

<Canvas of={VCardStories.LinkedGrid} />

## Disabled

`disabled` applies to a linked card: the link loses its address, is marked `aria-disabled`, and
the title and media are dimmed. The body stays readable, and controls in the slots are not
affected.

<Canvas of={VCardStories.Disabled} />

## Loading

`loading` replaces the title, subtitle and body with placeholders, and the media too when there is
one. The footer is hidden until the content arrives, and the card carries `aria-busy`. A loading
card is silent by default, so that a grid of them is not read out a dozen times; `loadingText`
announces it.

<Canvas of={VCardStories.Loading} />

## Accessibility

- The card is a `<div>` by default. `as` renders it as an `<article>`, a `<section>` or a `<li>`
  inside a list.
- A linked card has one link, its title. Wrapping the card in an `<a>` would name the link with
  all its text and could not contain buttons.
- The focus ring of the link is drawn around the whole card, the area it activates.
- Text inside a linked card cannot be selected by dragging over the stretched area; the controls
  and links in the slots can still be used.
- Images in `#media` need an `alt` text, or `alt=""` when they only decorate.
