# Tooltip

Tooltip on the Popover API (`popover="manual"`: no light dismiss, the component drives it). The
JavaScript is justified by the absence of a stable HTML primitive for "show on hover/focus with
a delay": it handles the opening delay, pointer/focus, the press on the trigger and Escape (WCAG 1.4.13). The anchoring is
100% CSS — a static `anchor-name` on the wrapper, confined to its subtree by `anchor-scope`,
with no unique ID and no inline style.

The trigger is supplied by the scoped default slot: setting `v-bind="triggerProps"` on the
focusable element attaches `aria-describedby` to it — the explicit route is preferred over an
automatic bridge at mount.

## Opening on focus

Hover opens after `delay` (300ms by default); **keyboard focus opens immediately** and Escape
closes without moving focus — in line with WCAG 1.4.13 (dismissable, hoverable, persistent).

## On a VIconButton

A typical use case: backing up an icon button's accessible label with a visual confirmation. The
tooltip describes (`aria-describedby`), it does not name — the VIconButton keeps its own `label`.

## Placements

Eight placements through `data-placement` → `position-area` (see `floating.css`, shared with
VPopover and VMenu). The aligned placements (`top-start`…) cancel the margin on the aligned side
so they stay flush with the trigger's edge.

## Rich content

The `#content` slot wins over the `text` prop. Reserved for **non-interactive** content
(formatting, `<kbd>`, icons): the tooltip closes as soon as the pointer leaves the trigger, and
`aria-describedby` flattens the content to text — a link or a button would be unreachable there.
For interactive content, use VPopover.

## Accessibility

- `role="tooltip"` + an `aria-describedby` set on the trigger through `triggerProps`: the content
  is announced when focus is taken.
- Keyboard focus: immediate opening (with no delay), closing through Escape or focus loss. It is
  the **keyboard** focus specifically, read from `:focus-visible`: a focus given by a click, or
  handed back to the trigger by a panel that is closing, opens nothing. Otherwise dismissing a
  menu would raise the tooltip over a page the reader has moved on from.
- **A tap opens no tooltip.** There is no hover on a touch screen, and a tooltip on tap would
  stand over whatever the tap just opened. Anything a touch reader needs has to be somewhere
  else as well: a visible label, a field hint, a panel.
- Pressing the trigger closes it too. That is what a trigger opening a panel needs: the pointer
  has not left and the focus may not have moved, so the tooltip would otherwise stand over what
  the click just opened.
- The tooltip complements, it does not replace: essential information must stay available
  otherwise (the control's label).
- The appearance animation (shared with `.v-floating`) respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
