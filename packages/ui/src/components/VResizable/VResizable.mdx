# Resizable

Panels that share a space, with a handle between each pair to resize them. VResizable holds two
or more `VResizablePanel`s and places the handles itself.

<Canvas of={VResizableStories.Default} />

<Controls of={VResizableStories.Default} />

## Sizes

The v-model holds the size of each panel in percent of the space they share, adding up to 100.
Left unbound, a panel starts from its `defaultSize`, and panels without one share what is left
equally. Sizes are rendered as flex weights, so a server-rendered page needs no measurement and
does not shift on hydration.

A handle is dragged with the pointer, or focused and moved with the arrow keys by `step` percent.

<Canvas of={VResizableStories.Drag} />

`orientation="vertical"` stacks the panels. The group then needs a height.

<Canvas of={VResizableStories.Vertical} />

## Limits

`minSize` and `maxSize` take a number in percent or a CSS length such as `240px` or `12rem`.
Lengths are converted against the group when a handle moves, and also written in CSS, so they
hold while the window is resized.

<Canvas of={VResizableStories.Limits} />

When a panel reaches its minimum, the handle takes the rest from the next panel along, and gives
space to the next panel once a neighbour reaches its maximum.

<Canvas of={VResizableStories.ThreePanels} />

## Collapsing

A `collapsible` panel collapses when a handle drags it past the middle of its minimum, or with
Enter on its handle, and reopens at the size it had. `v-model:collapsed` follows it and can
collapse or reopen it from outside, the other panels making room. `collapsedSize` keeps a strip,
such as an icon rail; at 0, the default, the content is removed from the layout and from the tab
order.

<Canvas of={VResizableStories.Collapsible} />

A handle reports on the panel before it, unless only the panel after it can collapse, as for a
sidebar on the end side.

<Canvas of={VResizableStories.CollapsibleEnd} />

## Nesting

A panel can hold a group of its own. Give the inner group `height: 100%`.

<Canvas of={VResizableStories.Nested} />

## Saving the sizes

The v-model changes on every step of a drag; `change` fires once the sizes settle, which is the
moment to save them. For a server-rendered page, a cookie lets the server render the saved sizes
at once.

## Accessibility

- Each handle is a focusable `separator`, as in the APG window splitter. `aria-valuenow`,
  `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax` give the size of its primary panel in percent, and
  `aria-controls` points at that panel.
- The handle is named after its primary panel's `label`, or "Panel 1" from the dictionary.
- The arrow keys along the group move the handle, following the direction of the text. Home and
  End give the primary panel its smallest and largest size, and Enter collapses or reopens it.
- A horizontal group has vertical handles, marked `aria-orientation="vertical"`.
- The line is a 1px border, visible in forced colours, and a wider invisible strip catches the
  pointer. `grip` adds a visible grip to every handle.
- A disabled group's handles leave the tab order and carry `aria-disabled`.
