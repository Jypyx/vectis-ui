# CommandPalette

A search field over a list of commands, in a native modal `<dialog>`: type to narrow the list,
walk it with the arrows, run a command with Enter. Like VDialog and VDrawer it is opened with
`showModal()` through `useModalDialog`, so the top layer, the backdrop, the focus trap, the inert
page and the focus returned to the trigger are all native. The palette sits near the top of the
viewport rather than in its centre, so the field stays still while the list changes height.

## Commands

`items` holds commands, named blocks of commands (`{ label, commands }`) and separators
(`{ separator: true }`). A command has a `label`, and optionally a `description` under it, an
`icon`, `keywords` the search also matches, and a `shortcut` shown at the end of its row through
VHotkeys. That shortcut is displayed only: the palette does not listen for it.

Choosing a command, by a click or by Enter, emits `select(command, event)` and closes the palette,
unless the command has `keepOpen`. A command with `href` is a real link: Enter and a click follow
it, and a modified or middle click opens it elsewhere while the palette stays open. With a router,
call `event.preventDefault()` in `select` and push the address. `id` is free for your own use and
comes back with the command.

## Searching

The search matches the label and the keywords, ignoring case and accents, and keeps the order of
`items`. A block with no match disappears, name included, and a separator left at an end or next
to another is not drawn. `filter` takes a rule of your own, or `false` when the source filters.

`v-model:query` holds what is typed, and is emptied each time the palette closes. `search`
reports it after `searchDebounce` milliseconds, and at once on opening; the same term is never
reported twice in a row. `loading` shows a spinner in the field, and a loading row while there is
nothing to show yet.

## Recent commands

The palette keeps no history. Record each choice from `select`, and while the query is empty, put
a block of the latest ones at the top of `items`.

## Custom rows

The `#item` slot receives `{ command, active }` and replaces the label and the description; the
icon and the shortcut stay in place. `#empty` receives the searched term, `#loading` replaces the
loading row and `#footer` the list of keys, which `hideFooter` removes. The list of keys is also
left out on touch-only devices, where no keyboard is expected; a custom `#footer` stays.

## Opening

`v-model:open` drives the palette and the `#trigger` slot hands out `triggerProps` for the button
that opens it. `shortcut` (`mod+k` for ⌘K on a Mac and Ctrl+K elsewhere) listens on the whole page
for a combination that opens the palette, and closes it again from its own field. It is ignored
while typing in another field, and adds `aria-keyshortcuts` to `triggerProps`. Escape and a click
on the backdrop close it. `show()`, `close()`, `focus()` and `el` are exposed through a `ref`.

## Accessibility

- The search field is a `combobox` controlling a `listbox`, and keeps the focus throughout:
  `aria-activedescendant` names the active command, which carries `aria-selected="true"`. Option
  ids follow the command rather than its position, so a filter sliding another command under the
  highlight is announced.
- `autofocus` puts the focus in the field when the dialog opens.
- The palette and its list are named by `label`, the field by `searchLabel`, from the design
  system dictionary by default.
- Blocks are `group`s named by their heading. Separators are presentational, a listbox owning only
  options and groups.
- Disabled commands carry `aria-disabled`, are skipped by the arrows and cannot be chosen.
- "No results" and the loading text are announced by a status region outside the listbox.
- The footer listing the keys is hidden from assistive technology, which the combobox already
  informs.
- The active row stays marked in forced colours, and the palette appears without transition under
  `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
