# Drawer

A modal panel pinned to one edge of the viewport, for filters, navigation or a detail view that
keeps the page in sight. Like VDialog it is a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()`: the top
layer, `::backdrop`, the focus trap, the inert page, the locked page scroll and the focus returned
to the trigger are all native. The two share their model bridge and their Safari fallback for
`closedby` (`useModalDialog`), and the same three zones: a header with its title, subtitle,
`#header-actions` and close cross, a scrolling body, and a `#footer`.

## Opening and closing

`v-model:open` drives the drawer, and the `#trigger` slot hands out `triggerProps` to bind on the
button that opens it. The cross, Escape and a click on the backdrop close it, and the `close`
event puts the model back in step. `persistentBackdrop` and `persistentEscape` restrict those
routes exactly as on VDialog. `show()`, `close()` and `el` are exposed through a `ref`.

The `<dialog>` is rendered only while it is needed. Unlike VDialog, it stays in the page after
closing until it has slid out: `display` and `overlay` are transitioned with
`transition-behavior: allow-discrete`, which keeps the closed drawer painted and in the top layer
for the duration of the exit, and VDrawer removes the element once
`getAnimations()` reports the transitions finished. Reopened during its exit, the same element
slides back in. The closed drawer lets clicks through to the page it uncovers.

Engines without the `overlay` property (Safari, Firefox) take the drawer out of the top layer as
soon as it closes: the backdrop disappears at once, and the drawer slides out above the page.

## Sides

`side` picks the edge: `start` and `end` follow the writing direction, `top` and `bottom` do not.
The corners against the viewport stay square; the two facing the page are rounded.

## Size

`size` sets the width of a drawer on a side, or the height of one at the top or bottom, from the
`--vectis-control-size-drawer-*` tokens. `extent` replaces it with any CSS length. Either way the
drawer leaves a strip of the page uncovered on a small screen, where the backdrop can still be
clicked.

## Overflowing content

The body scrolls while the header and the footer stay in place, and a hairline appears under the
header or above the footer once content passes under it (scroll-state container queries, where
supported).

## Accessibility

- The native `<dialog>` element carries the `dialog` role; the trigger receives
  `aria-haspopup="dialog"`.
- `title` and `subtitle` feed `aria-labelledby` and `aria-describedby`. With the `#header` slot,
  name the drawer yourself with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- `showModal()` provides the focus trap, the inert page and the focus return to the trigger.
- The cross is named by `closeLabel`, "Close" by default, from the design system dictionary.
- A body that scrolls is reached by the keyboard through the focusable content it holds.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the drawer and its backdrop appear and disappear without
  any transition.
