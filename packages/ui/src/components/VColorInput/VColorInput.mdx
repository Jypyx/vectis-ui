# ColorInput

A form field holding a colour. VColorInput is a VInput into which the colour can be typed, with a
swatch at its start that opens VColorPicker in a panel.

<Controls of={VColorInputStories.Default} />

## Value

`v-model` holds the colour written in `format` (`hex` by default, `rgb`, `hsl` or `oklch`), or
`null` when the field is empty. The field accepts all four formats; Enter or leaving it rewrites
what was typed in `format`, and anything that is not a colour puts the field back as it was.

## Picker

The swatch shows the current colour and opens the picker; the down arrow opens it from the field,
moving the focus to the saturation and brightness area. Escape, or Enter on a track or a swatch,
closes it and hands the focus back to the field, as does leaving the component. The picker writes
the value as it moves. It has no text field of its own: the field is the one above it.

<Canvas of={VColorInputStories.Keyboard} />

`alpha`, `swatches` and `hideEyeDropper` are passed to the picker. With `alpha`, the swatch shows
the opacity over a checkerboard.

## Field

VColorInput follows the field model: `label`, `hint`, and `error`, which replaces the hint, marks
the field invalid and is announced. Attributes such as `name` and `required` go to the text field.

`readonly` shows the colour without a picker; its swatch is no longer a button.

<Canvas of={VColorInputStories.ReadonlyAndDisabled} />

## Accessibility

- The field is a combobox with `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`
  pointing at the panel, a dialog named by `label` or "Choose a colour".
- The swatch is a button named "Open colour picker" from the dictionary, or `pickerLabel`.
- Enter in the field takes the typed colour and never opens the panel.
