# TimePicker

A time picker: a [VInput](?path=/docs/components-input--docs) field and, optionally, a
floating panel. Three ways of filling the field, chosen through the `mode` prop — a
**masked entry**, a **dial** or a **list of times**.

**By default the time is typed on the keyboard and there is no panel**: `mode` is `input`
and `showDial` is `false`.

The shell is the [VDatePicker](?path=/docs/components-datepicker--docs) one: a
`popover="manual"` panel anchored in **pure CSS** (`anchor-scope` on the root +
`anchor-name` on the control → `position-anchor`), a programmatic opening that moves focus
into the panel, and closing through the root's `focusout` + `Escape`.

Whatever the mode, the **v-model stays `'HH:mm'` in canonical 24 h** (`null` when empty):
picking 7 PM produces `'19:00'`.

## Three field modes

- **`input`** (the default): the field is typed on the keyboard, behind an "HH:MM" mask.
  The dial is optional (`showDial`) and absent by default.
- **`readonly`**: the field turns read-only and the dial becomes the only route —
  `showDial` is therefore forced to `true` there.
- **`list`**: a `listbox` panel offers the times in steps of `minuteStep`; the field stays
  read-only and `showDial` is beside the point there.

## Masked entry (the default)

The user types **digits only**: the colon is placed as soon as the hour is complete.
Unlike VDatePicker's mask, whose field order and separator derive from the locale, this
one is **universal** — `Intl` only varies the hour cycle, never the colon.

Entry rules:

- typing a separator is tolerated and completes the hour with a leading zero ("9:30" gives
  the same result as "0930"),
- `Backspace` on the colon erases the digit preceding it,
- an incomplete or impossible entry ("25:00") **silently reverts** to the current value on
  leaving the field; emptying the field sets the value to `null`,
- `Enter` confirms and closes, so it does not submit the surrounding form.

`showDial` adds the end-of-field icon and the dial: the panel then opens on focus
**without taking focus**, typing carries on in the field, and the `down arrow` stays the
explicit route to the dial.

`clockIcon` replaces that icon and only that one: in `mode="list"` the chevron follows the
VCombobox convention. `clearable`'s cross sits **to its left** without replacing it — the
cross plus the chevron, exactly like VCombobox.

## The dial

Two buttons display the draft time (hour / minutes) and choose the active step: the one
for the step under way switches to the accent tone. On the dial, a click or a drag
positions the hand; releasing after the hour has been chosen moves on to the minutes
automatically. The minutes are selected to the minute (the numerals are only 5-minute
markers); `minuteStep` imposes a granularity.

The panel works on a **draft**: only **OK** writes the v-model; **Cancel**, `Escape` and
focus leaving abandon the selection under way — you can feel your way around the dial
before confirming.

In 24 h, the dial carries a **double ring**: 12 and 1–11 on the outside, 00 and 13–23 on
the inside.

The numerals and the hand are positioned in **pure CSS** — `sin()`/`cos()` trigonometry
(CSS Values 4, well below the design system's browser floor) on a turn fraction set as an
inline variable. The JS is limited to the pointer → value conversion and the keyboard.

## The list of times

The `list` mode enumerates the day in steps of `minuteStep` (15 or 30 minutes are the
expected values; a finer step is flagged in development — at a 1-minute step the list
would be 1440 rows). On opening, the current value — or the closest one — has focus and
the list is scrolled onto it.

Choosing a time **commits immediately and closes**: picking from a list is a single
gesture, so there is neither a draft nor an OK button here.

## The 12 h / 24 h format and AM/PM

The hour cycle **derives from the locale** (`Intl`'s `hourCycle`) and is overridable
through the `format` prop. In 12 h, an AM/PM picker appears **next to the field**, outside
the panel: it therefore serves all three modes and writes the v-model directly, with
nothing to open or confirm. The dial itself goes back to a single ring.

As long as no time is set, the chosen meridiem is simply **remembered** and will apply to
the first time entered or picked. In `list` mode the row labels already carry "AM"/"PM":
the picker is then only there to switch the value that was kept.

The field displays the value formatted by `Intl` according to the locale.

## States

The field follows the input-component scale — `sm` (32px), `md` (40px, the default) and
`lg` (48px), combinable with `compact` (-4px); the panel keeps its dimensions — is
clearable (`clearable`: the cross appears to the left of the end icon as soon as a value
is set, including during an entry under way) and supports `disabled`. Cancel always
restores the last committed value.

## Accessibility

- When a panel exists, the field carries `aria-haspopup` (`dialog` for the dial, `listbox`
  for the list), `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. An input field with no `showDial`
  announces no popup.
- **Opening** on click, on `Enter` or on the `down arrow` from the field. In `readonly` and
  in `list`, focus moves into the panel at once; when typing with `showDial`, the panel
  opens **without taking focus**.
- The dial is a **single `role="slider"`** (one tab stop): arrows for ±1 hour or
  ±`minuteStep` minutes, `PageUp`/`PageDown` for ±5 minutes, `Home`/`End` for the bounds,
  `Enter` to settle the step. The dial's numerals are purely decorative (`aria-hidden`);
  the value is announced through `aria-valuetext` and the step change through a live
  region.
- The list is a `listbox` whose every row is a selectable `option`: arrows and `Home`/`End`
  move focus, `Enter` or `Space` pick.
- **Closing** through `Escape` or Cancel (focus returns to the field, with no commit),
  through OK, or — in `list` mode — by choosing a time.
- Since a placeholder is not an accessible description, describe the expected format
  through `hint` (linked to the field by `aria-describedby`).
- The dial is **not mirrored in RTL**: a clock is a universal object and clockwise is
  invariant; the HH:MM group likewise forces the hour–minutes visual order. Transitions
  respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

This component's accessible labels come from the design system dictionary: they are
translated globally (see **Foundations › Internationalization**). The `locale` prop stays
authoritative; without it, the component follows the global locale set by `setLocale`.
