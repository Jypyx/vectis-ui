# EmptyState

A static block that says there is nothing to show yet, and offers what to do about it: an icon in
a round badge, a title, a description and actions, centred.

<Canvas of={VEmptyStateStories.Default} />

<Controls of={VEmptyStateStories.Default} />

## Parts

- `icon` is drawn in a neutral round badge. The `#media` slot replaces the badge with an
  illustration of your own.
- `title` says what is empty in a few words.
- `description`, or the default slot for text with links, says why or what to do next.
- The `#actions` slot holds the buttons or links that get out of the empty state.

Every part is optional and leaves no space when absent.

## Title and heading level

The title is a paragraph by default, which keeps an empty panel out of the document outline.
`headingLevel` renders it as `<h1>` to `<h6>` without changing how it looks, for an empty state
that stands for a whole page or section.

<Canvas of={VEmptyStateStories.HeadingLevel} />

## Sizes

`size` scales the badge (`--vectis-control-size-empty-state-media-*`), the spacing and the
title: `sm` for a panel or a list, `md` for a section, `lg` for a whole page. The title and the
description wrap at `--vectis-control-size-empty-state-text-max`.

<Canvas of={VEmptyStateStories.Sizes} />

## In VDataTable and VCombobox

Both components show a small VEmptyState when they have nothing to list. Its title is their
`emptyText`. Its icon is a crossed-out magnifier after a search that found nothing, and an inbox
otherwise. Their `#empty` slot still replaces it, for instance with a VEmptyState that has
actions.

<Canvas of={VEmptyStateStories.InDataTable} />

## Accessibility

- The badge icon is decorative and hidden from assistive technology: the title carries the
  meaning.
- The block has no live role. A component that empties as the reader types announces the change
  itself, as VCombobox does.
- With `headingLevel`, check that the level fits the headings around the block.
