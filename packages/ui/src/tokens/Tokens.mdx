# Tokens

This page is **generated from the typed source** (`src/tokens/`) — it cannot diverge
from the code. Components only consume the **semantic** tokens; the primitives are
there only to define them.

Runtime override: redefine any `--vectis-*` custom property on `:root` or on a DOM
subtree (no rebuild). Dark mode: `data-theme="dark"`.

## Primitive palettes

The palettes are Tailwind CSS 4's, family for family and step for step. There are exactly
five, and they are exactly the five a role points at: **gray** paints the surfaces, the text
and the borders, **indigo** is the default accent, and **red**, **green** and **amber**
carry danger, success and warning.

Nothing ships that nothing paints with. A palette costs eleven custom properties in every
page that loads the stylesheet, read or not, so a sixth colour is declared by the
application: eleven steps under its own name, in its own unlayered stylesheet, and the role
pointed at them. That is the whole of it — no rebuild, no release. The documentation site
does it for its violet accent.

Do not confuse `gray` — the greys this design system paints with, very slightly blue — with
`tone="neutral"`, which is a role on a component and not a palette.

### Semantic scale (text roles)

Every role is a complete recipe (`--vectis-text-<role>-size/weight/leading[/tracking]`),
consumed by the **VTypography** component and by the components' CSS. The `control` role
is technical (the labels of interactive controls): its size comes from the control size
scale, not from a token of its own.

<div style={{ display: 'grid', gap: 12, margin: '8px 0 24px' }}>
  {Object.entries(tokens.semantic.text)
    .filter(([, node]) => node.$value === undefined)
    .map(([role]) => (
      <div key={role} style={{ display: 'flex', alignItems: 'baseline', gap: 12 }}>
        <code style={{ ...mono, width: 140, flexShrink: 0 }}>{role}</code>
        <span
          style={{
            fontFamily: `var(--vectis-text-family${
              role === 'code'
                ? '-code'
                : role === 'display' || role.startsWith('heading')
                  ? '-heading'
                  : ''
            })`,
            fontSize: `var(--vectis-text-${role}-size, var(--vectis-font-size-sm))`,
            fontWeight: `var(--vectis-text-${role}-weight)`,
            lineHeight: `var(--vectis-text-${role}-leading)`,
            letterSpacing: `var(--vectis-text-${role}-tracking, normal)`,
          }}
        >
          The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog
        </span>
      </div>
    ))}
</div>

Semantic families: `--vectis-text-family` (running text), `--vectis-text-family-heading`
(the `display` and `heading-1…4` roles) and `--vectis-text-family-code` (code content).
The heading one points at the running one out of the box, so giving titles a typeface of
their own is a matter of redefining that single property.

## Motion

Three roles say how long a change takes. They are what a `transition` asks for, and they
are the same in both themes.

skeleton's wave — names a step directly instead: "one second" is the whole point of such a
loop, and no role would say it better.
